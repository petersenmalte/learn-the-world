    A WORLD TO DISCOVER

    Your pocket atlas.

    Explore countries, capitals, and natural features worldwide on a globe made from open geographic data.

    Coverage. 242 Natural Earth countries and map units, including territories and disputed areas. 241 GeoNames capital records (PPLC). These are source-defined lists, not a count of sovereign states or every seat of government.

    Worldwide physical places. Search includes the GeoNames world snapshot for mountains, peaks, ranges, volcanoes, glaciers, icecaps, rivers and streams (including intermittent ones), lakes, deserts, seas and oceans. Counts are source records, not a claim that every feature on Earth is catalogued. Extra Natural Earth shapes provide regional context.

    At a world view, the globe shows an overview. Zoom in for local names; search can find every included record regardless of zoom or layer visibility. Many records are location markers: river courses, lake boundaries and glacier extents are only drawn where shape data is available.

    Controls. Drag or use arrow keys to explore. Scroll, pinch, or use + / − to zoom. Press / to search; use ↑ / ↓ and Enter to choose a result. Escape closes search. Search includes hidden layers. Start with a word in the place’s name; case and accents do not matter.

    Data: Natural Earth (public domain) and GeoNames, adapted under CC BY 4.0. Snapshot: 2 October 2026. All map data is served with this app.
